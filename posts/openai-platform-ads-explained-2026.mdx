OpenAI just launched its own advertising platform inside ChatGPT — and it is bigger news than most people realise.

As per the latest updates, they have started showing ads very early. The platform is still fresh, still being tested, and moving fast.

Right now it is only available in the **USA**. Countries like India cannot access it yet — but it is coming.

---

## Who Can Run These Ads?

Both businesses and individuals can sign up — but currently only businesses are fully operational on the platform. Individual advertisers are still waiting for full access.

One interesting thing — no third party agencies are allowed to run ads on your behalf through the self-serve platform. You have to do it yourself as a business. Though OpenAI has partnered with a few large agency groups separately, the self-serve tool is business-direct only.

---

## Why Is OpenAI Doing This Right Now?

You might be wondering — why launch ads so early before everything is perfectly set up?

The honest reason is money. OpenAI's expenses are enormous. Running ChatGPT for nearly a billion users costs billions every year. Revenue from subscriptions alone is not enough to cover it.

There is also the investor angle. OpenAI needs to show its investors that the business is growing and generating real revenue. Launching an ad platform — even in early beta — sends a strong signal that ChatGPT can be monetised at scale.

---

## How Does It Work? The Campaign Structure

Just like Google Ads and Meta Ads, the OpenAI platform divides campaigns into three levels:

1. **Campaign** — Your overall goal and budget
2. **Ad Group** — Who you are targeting
3. **Ad** — The actual message users see

Clean, familiar, and straightforward if you have run ads before.

---

## Two Ways To Pay — CPC and CPM

**CPM** — You pay for every 1000 people who see your ad.
Current rate: **$60 per 1000 impressions**
Best for: Brand awareness

**CPC** — You pay only when someone actually clicks.
Recommended bid: **$3 to $5 per click**
Best for: Getting real traffic and leads

---

## Strict Ad Policies

OpenAI takes content very seriously. You cannot run ads related to:

- Alcohol or drugs
- Adult content
- Gambling
- Politics
- Health and mental health topics
- Anything targeting minors

The platform is stricter than Google and Meta in several areas. OpenAI wants to protect the trust users have in ChatGPT — and that means keeping ads relevant and clean.

---

## Your Privacy

Advertisers never see your conversations. They only receive overall campaign stats — like how many people clicked. OpenAI does not sell your data. You can turn off ad personalisation anytime. And if you are on Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise plans — you will not see any ads at all.

OpenAI's ad platform is early, US-only for now, and still growing. But it is real, it is live, and it is already making money. By the time it reaches India and the rest of the world — the businesses that understood it early will have a serious advantage.
