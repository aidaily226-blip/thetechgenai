# Key Features of ECOVACS Robot Vacuum Cleaners

ECOVACS DEEBOT models differ considerably, but several technologies appear across the company's range.

## 1. Vacuuming and Mopping

Many DEEBOT models combine vacuuming and mopping in one device.

The robot can collect dry debris while also cleaning hard floors with a wet mopping system.

This is particularly useful for households that want to automate routine floor maintenance.

However, robot mopping should not be considered a complete replacement for deep manual cleaning.

---

## 2. Strong Suction

Different DEEBOT models provide different levels of suction power.

For example, ECOVACS lists 4,300Pa suction for the older N10 and 11,000Pa for the T30 Pro Omni.

Newer premium models can offer substantially higher specifications.

Higher suction can help with dust, debris and hair, but suction power alone does not determine how well a robot cleans.

Brush design, navigation, floor type, airflow and cleaning frequency also matter.

---

## 3. ZeroTangle Technology

Hair can become wrapped around conventional robot vacuum brushes.

ECOVACS uses its **ZeroTangle** technology in several DEEBOT models to reduce hair accumulation around the brush.

This can be particularly useful for homes with long hair or pets.

The implementation differs between generations, so buyers should check the specifications of the exact model rather than assuming every DEEBOT has the same ZeroTangle system.

---

## 4. Smart Navigation

Navigation is one of the most important features of a robot vacuum.

A robot needs to understand where it has already cleaned and where it still needs to go.

DEEBOT models use technologies such as laser-based mapping, sensors and cameras depending on the model.

ECOVACS says its systems can create maps of the home and plan cleaning routes automatically.

This allows users to select rooms, create schedules and manage cleaning through the app on supported models.

---

## 5. Obstacle Detection

Objects such as cables, furniture, shoes and other household items can make robot vacuum navigation difficult.

More advanced DEEBOT models use additional sensors and cameras to recognize obstacles and adjust their route.

The performance varies by model.

Therefore, a buyer should not assume that every ECOVACS robot has the same level of obstacle recognition.

# What Is an OMNI Station?

One of the biggest differences between basic and premium robot vacuums is the charging station.

A basic robot may simply return to its dock to recharge.

An advanced **OMNI station** can perform several maintenance tasks.

Depending on the DEEBOT model, the station may:

- Empty the robot's dustbin
- Wash the mop
- Refill water
- Dry the mop
- Recharge the robot

This reduces the amount of manual maintenance required from the owner.

However, an OMNI station is larger than a simple charging dock and requires additional floor space.

# How Does an ECOVACS Robot Vacuum Work?

The basic cleaning process is relatively simple.

### Step 1: Mapping

The robot scans its environment and creates a map of the cleaning area.

### Step 2: Planning

It calculates a route based on the available space and obstacles.

### Step 3: Vacuuming

The brushes and suction system collect dust, hair and other debris.

### Step 4: Mopping

On compatible models, the robot uses its mopping system to clean hard floors.

### Step 5: Returning to the Station

When the cleaning cycle is finished or the battery needs charging, the robot can return to its station.

Advanced models can perform additional maintenance automatically.

# ECOVACS vs a Traditional Vacuum Cleaner

A robot vacuum and a traditional vacuum cleaner serve slightly different purposes.

A traditional vacuum gives the user direct control over where and how the cleaning happens.

A robot vacuum is designed to perform regular cleaning automatically.

For example, you can schedule a DEEBOT to clean while you are working or away from home.

However, a traditional vacuum is still useful for:

- Stairs
- Corners
- Sofas
- Curtains
- Cars
- Deep cleaning
- Areas the robot cannot reach

For many households, the two can work together rather than one completely replacing the other.
