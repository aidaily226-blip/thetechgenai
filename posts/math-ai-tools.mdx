## 1. Khanmigo by Khan Academy

**Best for: Students from Class 4 to Class 12 who want a patient AI tutor**

**Website:** khanmigo.ai

Khan Academy has been around forever. Khanmigo is what happens when you plug a proper AI tutor into all of that content.

What makes it different from every other tool on this list is the philosophy. Khanmigo will not just solve your problem. It asks you questions back. It nudges you toward the answer. It figures out where your understanding broke down and explains exactly that part — not the whole topic from scratch.

It covers everything from basic arithmetic to calculus, statistics, and SAT math prep.

The AI is trained to be a Socratic tutor — meaning it teaches through questions, not through answers. That is a genuinely different experience from most Math AI tools.

**Free or Paid:** Free for students in many regions. Some premium features through Khan Academy's school programme.

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

---

## 2. Mathway

**Best for: College students who need step-by-step solutions across advanced topics**

**Website:** mathway.com

Mathway has been around longer than most AI math tools but it has aged well. Type or photograph your problem — algebra, trigonometry, calculus, linear algebra, chemistry math, statistics — and it breaks down every single step.

Where it earns its place on this list is depth. It does not just do basic school maths. It handles university-level problems that most other tools fumble on.

The free version shows the final answer. The paid version shows every step — and that is the version worth paying for if you are in college.

**Free or Paid:** Free for answers, paid for full steps (around $9.99/month)

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

---

## 3. Photomath

**Best for: Visual learners who want to scan and learn**

**Website:** photomath.net

Point your phone camera at any handwritten or printed math problem and Photomath reads it, solves it, and explains every step with animations.

The camera scanning is genuinely impressive — it handles messy handwriting better than most humans can read it.

What keeps it on this list is the explanation quality. Each step has a written explanation of why that step was taken — not just what the answer is. For students who learn by seeing the process visually laid out, this is one of the best tools available.

It covers pre-algebra through calculus.

**Free or Paid:** Free with ads, Photomath Plus from $9.99/month for detailed explanations

**Works on:** iOS, Android

---

## 4. Wolfram Alpha

**Best for: Advanced students, engineers, and anyone working with complex maths**

**Website:** wolframalpha.com

Wolfram Alpha is not marketed as an AI tutor but it is one of the most powerful math computation engines ever built — and it has been quietly adding AI explanation layers for years.

Type in any mathematical expression — from basic fractions to differential equations to number theory — and it gives you the answer, a graph, alternate forms, step-by-step solutions, and related concepts.

Where it stands out is breadth. No other tool on this list handles the sheer range of mathematical topics that Wolfram Alpha covers. It also integrates with Mathematica for users who need professional-grade computation.

For self-learners doing anything beyond high school maths, this is essential.

**Free or Paid:** Free for basic results, Wolfram Alpha Pro from $7.99/month for full steps

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

## 5. Gauthmath

**Best for: Students who want instant AI help plus real human tutors on standby**

**Website:** gauthmath.com

Gauthmath is interesting because it blends AI with real human math tutors. You snap a photo of your problem. The AI attempts to solve it and explain it. If the AI cannot handle it — or if you want a human to verify — you can connect with a live tutor.

That hybrid model is genuinely useful for tricky problems where you are not sure if the AI explanation is correct.

It covers school-level maths across most curricula including CBSE, ICSE, and international boards — which makes it particularly useful for Indian students.

**Free or Paid:** Free with limited daily questions, paid plans for unlimited access

**Works on:** iOS, Android

## 6. Microsoft Math Solver

**Best for: Students who want a completely free, no-nonsense AI math tool**

**Website:** math.microsoft.com

Microsoft Math Solver does not get enough credit. It is completely free, has no paywalled steps, handles a wide range of topics from basic to advanced, and the explanations are clear and genuinely useful.

You can type, draw, or scan your problem. It shows step-by-step solutions, links to related video tutorials, and offers a graphing calculator built in.

For students in India or anywhere else who cannot afford paid subscriptions — this is the best free Math AI tool available right now. Full stop.

**Free or Paid:** Completely free

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

## 7. Numerade

**Best for: Students preparing for competitive exams like JEE, SAT, ACT, or AP exams**

**Website:** numerade.com

Numerade is less of a solver and more of an AI-powered video explanation platform. It has millions of step-by-step video solutions to textbook problems — and an AI tutor called Ace that can answer follow-up questions on any topic.

Where it shines is exam prep. The content is specifically designed around popular textbooks and exam formats. If you are preparing for JEE Mains, JEE Advanced, SAT Math, or AP Calculus — the chances that your exact problem or a very similar one already has a detailed video explanation on Numerade are very high.

**Free or Paid:** Free for limited access, paid plans from $9.99/month

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

## 8. Symbolab

**Best for: Students who want to learn the process, not just the answer**

**Website:** symbolab.com

Symbolab has built its entire product around one idea: show your work.

Every solution comes with a detailed step-by-step breakdown written in plain language. It covers algebra, calculus, trigonometry, matrices, and more. The practice problem generator is one of its best features — it creates problems at your level and checks your working, not just your final answer.

For students who have exams where showing working is required — which is basically every board exam in India — Symbolab is one of the most practical tools available.

**Free or Paid:** Free for basic solutions, paid from $2.99/month for full steps

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

## 9. Socratic by Google

**Best for: School students who want quick visual explanations**

**Website:** socratic.org (also on app stores)

Google built Socratic as a homework helper and it is particularly strong on math. Take a photo of your problem and it searches for the best explanation across the web — pulling from Khan Academy, YouTube videos, and educational resources — then presents them in a clean visual format.

It is not as deep as some other tools for complex maths. But for middle school and high school students who want a quick, visually friendly explanation, it is excellent.

And it is completely free.

**Free or Paid:** Completely free

**Works on:** iOS, Android

## 10. ChatGPT or Claude with Maths Prompts

**Best for: Students who want to have a proper back-and-forth conversation about maths**

**Website:** chatgpt.com / claude.ai

Worth including because of how many students are already using general-purpose AI assistants for maths — and often not using them well.

Both ChatGPT and Claude can explain mathematical concepts at any level, work through problems step by step, adapt explanations to your understanding, and answer follow-up questions naturally.

The trick is prompting them properly. Do not just paste the problem. Tell the AI your level, where you are confused, and ask it to explain step by step. Ask it to quiz you afterward. Used correctly, a general AI assistant can be one of the best math tutors available.

The weakness is that they can sometimes make arithmetic errors on very complex calculations — so always verify final numerical answers with a dedicated tool like Wolfram Alpha.

**Free or Paid:** Free tiers available, paid plans for more usage

**Works on:** Web, iOS, Android

## How to Actually Use Math AI Tools to Learn — Not Cheat

This is important enough to say clearly.

If you use a Math AI tool to copy an answer and move on, you will fail your exam. The tool did the learning. You did not.

The way these tools actually help is:

**Step 1** — Attempt the problem yourself first. Even if you get it wrong.

**Step 2** — Use the AI to show you the solution step by step.

**Step 3** — Read every step. Identify the exact step where your approach was different.

**Step 4** — Close the tool and try a similar problem from memory.

**Step 5** — Repeat.

That is the difference between using AI to learn maths and using AI to avoid learning maths. The first one works. The second one does not.

## Quick FAQs

**What is the best Math AI app in 2026?**

For most students, Microsoft Math Solver is the best free option. For paid tools, Khanmigo offers the best actual tutoring experience, and Wolfram Alpha is the most powerful for advanced maths.

**Can AI really teach maths or does it just give answers?**

The best Math AI tools — Khanmigo, Symbolab, Photomath, and Mathway — are specifically designed to teach through step-by-step explanations rather than just returning answers. Used correctly, they are effective learning tools.

**Is using Math AI cheating?**

Using Math AI to understand how to solve a problem is not cheating — it is studying. Using it to copy answers without engaging with the process is cheating, and it will show up in your exam performance.

**Which Math AI tool works for Indian students and Indian curriculum?**

Gauthmath supports CBSE and ICSE curricula specifically. Microsoft Math Solver and Photomath also work well for Indian school syllabi. Numerade is strong for JEE and competitive exam preparation.

**Are there free Math AI tools that show steps?**

Yes. Microsoft Math Solver shows full steps completely free. Socratic by Google is also free. Khan Academy's Khanmigo is free for students in many regions.
