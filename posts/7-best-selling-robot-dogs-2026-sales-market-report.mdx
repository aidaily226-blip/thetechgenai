7 Best Robot Dogs in 2026: Sales, Prices, Features and Market Leaders

Robot dogs are no longer just experimental machines walking around technology exhibitions. They are increasingly being used for industrial inspections, security, research, education, transportation and even entertainment.

Companies in China, the United States and Europe are developing four-legged robots that can walk across difficult terrain, climb stairs, carry equipment and navigate environments without constant human control.

The commercial market is also growing rapidly. According to Counterpoint Research, global quadruped robot shipments approached 35,000 units in the first half of 2026, with approximately 40% intended for industrial applications.

But which companies are selling the most robot dogs? Which models are attracting commercial customers? And which robots are worth watching?

Here are seven important robot dogs and quadruped platforms in 2026, along with available sales and market-share evidence.

## Robot Dog Market in 2026: Sales and Demand

Before looking at individual robots, it is important to understand the market.

Quadruped robots are machines designed with four legs instead of wheels. Their structure allows them to move through environments where conventional wheeled robots may struggle.

Their applications include:

- Industrial inspection and equipment monitoring.
- Security patrols and surveillance.
- Firefighting and emergency response.
- Research and robotics education.
- Entertainment and consumer companionship.
- Transportation of equipment across difficult terrain.

### Global robot dog shipment figures

According to Counterpoint Research, global quadruped robot shipments approached 35,000 units in the first half of 2026.

| Market indicator | Reported figure |
|---|---:|
| Global shipments, H1 2026 | Nearly 35,000 |
| Industrial applications | Approximately 40% |
| Unitree global shipment share | 37% |
| AGIQUAD global shipment share | 16.3% |
| GENISOM AI global shipment share | 12.6% |
| DEEP Robotics global shipment share | 10.4% |

These percentages refer to global quadruped robot shipments, not revenue or retail sales.

The figures also show that Chinese manufacturers have become important competitors in the global robot dog industry.

## 7 Best Robot Dogs in 2026

The following selection combines documented sales, shipment performance, commercial deployment and technological importance.

**Important:** This is not a verified worldwide ranking of seven individual models by unit sales. Most manufacturers do not publish model-by-model sales figures. Where company-level sales data is available, it is identified separately.

## 1. Unitree Go2 – Unitree Robotics

**Country:** China

**Category:** Consumer, research and education

Unitree Go2 is one of the most recognisable robot dogs in the consumer and research markets.

It is designed to walk, run, follow commands and navigate different environments. Its relatively accessible price compared with industrial robot platforms has helped make Unitree a major name in quadruped robotics.

### Important features

- Advanced four-legged movement.
- Intelligent obstacle avoidance.
- LiDAR-based environmental perception on supported configurations.
- Remote control and mobile application support.
- Autonomous following and navigation capabilities.
- Programmable development environment.
- Support for robotics research and experimentation.

### Sales performance

Unitree reported selling **23,037 quadruped robots in 2025**, according to figures from its listing documents.

This is a company-wide quadruped sales figure, not the confirmed sales number for Go2 alone. The company's product portfolio includes several quadruped models.

In the first half of 2026, Unitree held a reported 37% share of global quadruped robot shipments, according to Counterpoint Research.

This makes Unitree one of the most commercially important robot dog manufacturers.

## 2. Vbot SuperDog – Vbot

**Country:** China

**Category:** Consumer and personal assistance

Vbot SuperDog is an interesting example of a robot dog designed for everyday consumers rather than only factories and research laboratories.

Its focus is on mobility, following people and carrying lightweight equipment.

### Important features

- Autonomous person-following.
- LiDAR and ultra-wideband positioning technology.
- Obstacle avoidance.
- Maximum reported payload of 12 kg.
- Ability to carry groceries and outdoor equipment.
- Support for carrying an action camera.
- Expandable carrying platform.

### Sales performance

Vbot announced that its cumulative global quadruped robot sales had exceeded **13,000 units by September 8, 2026**.

The company also reported receiving 6,540 pre-orders when pre-orders opened in December 2025.

It announced more than 5,000 additional orders during IFA 2026 through agreements with 16 international partners.

These are company-reported figures, so they should be understood as manufacturer announcements rather than independently audited global market totals.

Vbot is particularly interesting because it demonstrates that robot dogs are moving beyond industrial applications and entering consumer markets.

## Why Are Robot Dogs Becoming More Popular?

There are several reasons behind the increasing demand for quadruped robots.

### 1. Industrial automation

Factories and energy companies need to inspect equipment regularly. Robot dogs can collect information from locations where routine human inspection may be difficult.

### 2. Security and surveillance

Quadruped robots can carry cameras and sensors to monitor selected environments.

They can support security teams, although they do not automatically replace human security personnel.

### 3. Dangerous environments

Some industrial locations contain heat, chemicals, difficult terrain or other hazards.

Robots can help collect information while reducing the need for workers to enter certain areas.

### 4. Research and education

Universities and robotics laboratories use quadruped platforms to study artificial intelligence, motion control, navigation and machine perception.

### 5. Consumer interest

Consumer robot dogs are becoming more capable of following people, carrying objects and interacting with their surroundings.

This could create a new category of personal robotic devices.

## What Is the Future of Robot Dogs?

The next generation of quadruped robots could become more useful through improvements in AI and autonomous decision-making.

Some important developments include:

- AI-powered object recognition.
- More reliable autonomous navigation.
- Better battery performance.
- Robotic arms and additional payload systems.
- Improved communication with human operators.
- Integration with industrial monitoring software.
- More advanced inspection and emergency-response capabilities.

The market may also become more competitive as manufacturers reduce production costs and develop robots for specific industries.

The biggest opportunity could be in industrial automation, where businesses can measure the value of robots through inspection efficiency, reduced downtime and improved worker safety.

## Final Thoughts

Robot dogs are becoming a serious part of the global robotics industry.

Unitree has demonstrated strong sales and shipment performance, Vbot is pushing into the consumer market, and companies such as DEEP Robotics, AGIQUAD and GENISOM AI are expanding industrial applications.

Meanwhile, Boston Dynamics Spot and ANYmal continue to represent important professional inspection platforms.

The industry is moving beyond robots that simply walk and perform demonstrations. The next challenge is making them useful, reliable and affordable enough for regular commercial operations.
