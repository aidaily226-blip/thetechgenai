## How Does the Unitree Go2 Pro Work?

The basic operation of the robot involves several systems working together.

### Step 1: Sensing the Environment

The robot uses sensors such as LiDAR and cameras to understand its surroundings.

### Step 2: Processing Information

Its onboard computing system processes information from the sensors.

### Step 3: Planning Movement

The robot determines how it should move while maintaining balance.

### Step 4: Leg Movement

The motors in its legs execute the movement commands.

### Step 5: Navigation

The robot continuously uses its sensors to react to its environment while moving.

This combination of sensing, computing and motor control is what allows the Go2 Pro to move more naturally than a basic remote-controlled robot.
