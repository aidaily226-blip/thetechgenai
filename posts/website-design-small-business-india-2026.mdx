## What Does A Small Business Website Actually Need?

You do not need anything fancy. Here is what matters:

**1. Mobile Friendly Design**

If you are planning a new business website in 2026, mobile-first design must be your foundation — not an afterthought. A slow-loading site feels unreliable and unprofessional, no matter how beautiful it looks. A few seconds of delay can make visitors leave instantly.

In simple words — your website must look good and load fast on a phone. That is the single most important thing.

**2. Your Basic Information — Clear and Easy to Find**

- What you do
- Where you are located
- How to contact you
- Your timings

Most customers just want these four things. Give them quickly and they call you.

**3. WhatsApp Button**

In India, especially in the Delhi NCR region, WhatsApp is the primary communication tool. Integrating direct click-to-chat features is essential for converting traffic into leads.

Every Indian business website needs a WhatsApp button. It is the fastest way to convert a visitor into a customer. One tap and they are talking to you directly.

**4. Google Maps Integration**

Add your Google Maps location to the website. Customers can click and get directions immediately. Very simple. Very effective.

**5. Fast Loading Speed**

Research confirms that improving site speed by as little as 0.1 seconds can boost conversion rates by up to 8 percent. Compress all images before uploading.

A slow website is worse than no website. If your site takes more than 3 seconds to load on mobile — most people leave before seeing anything.

## What To Ask Before Hiring Someone To Build Your Website

Before you pay anyone — ask these questions:

**1. Will my website work on mobile?**
If they hesitate — move on.

**2. How fast will it load?**
Should be under 3 seconds on mobile.

**3. Will you set up Google My Business for me?**
This is what puts you on Google Maps. Essential.

**4. Will I own the website or will it be on your platform?**
You should own it completely. Some agencies host it themselves and you lose everything if you stop paying them.

**5. Can I see examples of websites you have built?**
Any serious developer has a portfolio.

**6. What happens after the website is built — do you provide support?**
Good agencies offer at least 30 days of free support after delivery.
