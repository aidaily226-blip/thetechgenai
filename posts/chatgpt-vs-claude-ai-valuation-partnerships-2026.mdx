## First — Who Makes These AIs?

**ChatGPT** is made by **OpenAI** — founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk (who later left), and others. Based in San Francisco. The company that started the modern AI revolution with the launch of ChatGPT in November 2022.

**Claude AI** is made by **Anthropic** — founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and several other former OpenAI researchers. Also based in San Francisco. Built specifically with a safety-first approach to AI.

Both companies are privately held. Neither has a publicly traded stock yet — though both are racing toward IPOs.

## The Valuation — Who Is Worth More?

This is where the story gets really interesting.

<cite index="24-1">On March 31, 2026, OpenAI closed a $122 billion funding round at an $852 billion post-money valuation — the largest private financing in history.</cite>

$852 billion. Nearly a trillion dollars. For a company that has never made a profit.

Impressive? Absolutely.

But here is the twist.

<cite index="31-1">Anthropic — the company behind Claude — raised $65 billion in Series H funding in May 2026, valuing the company at $965 billion post-money.</cite>

$965 billion. That is $113 billion more than OpenAI.

<cite index="27-1">Fourteen months ago OpenAI was seeking a $230 billion valuation. Today it is $852 billion, Anthropic is at $965 billion.</cite>

In simple words — Claude AI's parent company just overtook ChatGPT's parent company in valuation. The underdog is now worth more.

## Revenue — Who Is Actually Making More Money?

This is where the real story is.

**OpenAI / ChatGPT:**
<cite index="27-1">OpenAI closed 2025 at $21.4 billion in revenue and reached approximately $25 billion by mid-2026. About $17 billion comes from ChatGPT subscriptions, $6.5 billion from API usage, and $1.5 billion from Sora video and licensing.</cite>

**Anthropic / Claude:**
<cite index="31-1">Anthropic's run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion earlier in May 2026.</cite>

Wait. Let that sink in.

Claude's revenue is nearly double ChatGPT's revenue. A company most regular people have barely heard of is generating more money from AI than the most famous AI in the world.

<cite index="26-1">April 2026 reporting cited analysis suggesting Anthropic's enterprise revenue had overtaken OpenAI's enterprise revenue, with more than 1,000 Anthropic enterprise customers spending $1 million or more on Claude annually.</cite>

More than 1,000 companies each spending over $1 million per year on Claude. That is a very different kind of business than selling $20/month subscriptions.

## The Profitability Problem — The Big Liability

Here is the uncomfortable truth about both companies.

Neither of them is profitable.

Not even close.

**OpenAI's finances:**
<cite index="27-1">OpenAI runs at an estimated -122% operating margin — it loses about $1.22 for every dollar of revenue. OpenAI does not turn cash-flow positive until 2030. Projected burn is approximately $27 billion in 2026 and approximately $63 billion in 2027.</cite>

$63 billion in losses in 2027 alone. For one company.

The reason is simple — AI is incredibly expensive to run. Every ChatGPT query needs massive GPU computing power. Every GPU needs enormous amounts of electricity. And OpenAI has hundreds of millions of users.

**Anthropic's situation:**
Anthropic has similar infrastructure costs. Building and running AI at this scale costs billions regardless of how much revenue you make. Anthropic has not publicly disclosed its burn rate but the pattern is the same — spending more than earning, banking on future dominance.

Both companies are essentially betting — spending billions now on the assumption that AI will be so central to the world economy that they will eventually dominate it and make all the money back.

It is the boldest bet in business history. And nobody knows yet if it pays off.

Here is what most people talking about Claude AI completely miss.

While ChatGPT is famous for its consumer app — the website and mobile app that regular people use — Claude has been quietly embedding itself into the software that runs the world's biggest companies.

This is the real story of 2026.

<cite index="35-1">Anthropic unveiled 10 high-profile integrations for its Claude platform, partnering with heavyweights such as Salesforce, DocuSign, and FactSet, positioning Claude as the cognitive engine behind the tools that corporations already use.</cite>

Let us go through the major partnerships:

**Amazon** — The biggest and most important. <cite index="28-1">As part of "Project Rainier," Amazon built a vast network of data centers and custom AI chips specifically optimized for Anthropic's workloads. Claude is deeply integrated into Amazon Bedrock, AWS's managed AI service.</cite>

**Google** — <cite index="28-1">Google invested billions in Anthropic and the collaboration focuses on compute access and cloud integration. Claude is accessible on Google Vertex AI, making it easy for customers using Google Cloud to add Claude to their workflows.</cite>

**Microsoft** — <cite index="34-1">Microsoft has integrated Anthropic's models into Office 365 Copilot, extending Claude-powered AI across Microsoft's productivity apps and potentially reaching more than 100 million users.</cite>

**Salesforce** — The world's largest CRM software. Claude is now embedded in Salesforce products used by sales teams at thousands of companies globally.

**ServiceNow** — <cite index="36-1">ServiceNow deepened its partnership with Anthropic, integrating Claude to power its Build Agent — the AI tool for building applications and workflows, letting both professional and non-technical workers create software using plain language.</cite>

**Slack** — The workplace messaging tool used by millions of companies. Claude integration means employees can get AI assistance without leaving Slack.

**DocuSign** — Used for digital contracts and signatures across thousands of businesses globally. Claude is now embedded in their document intelligence features.

**Databricks** — <cite index="34-1">Claude is accessible on Databricks, making it easy for data teams to integrate Claude into existing analytics workflows.</cite>

**Samsung, Micron, SK hynix** — <cite index="31-1">Strategic infrastructure partners whose technologies play a critical role in the world's supply of memory, storage, and logic chips, helping Anthropic scale compute reliably.</cite>

And beyond individual companies — <cite index="37-1">Anthropic invested $100 million into the Claude Partner Network in 2026, providing training, technical support, and joint market development for partners helping enterprises adopt Claude.</cite>

The strategy is brilliant. Instead of competing with ChatGPT for individual users — Claude is becoming the invisible AI brain inside the software that businesses already use every day.

## Which One Is Actually Better To Use?

This is the question everyone asks. And the honest answer is — it depends on what you are doing.

**Use ChatGPT if:**
- You want the most well-known AI with the biggest user community
- You need image generation (DALL-E is integrated)
- You use it casually for everyday tasks
- You want the widest range of third-party plugins and integrations for consumers

**Use Claude if:**
- You do a lot of writing, analysis, or complex thinking
- You need accurate, nuanced answers without the AI making things up
- You are working with long documents — Claude handles much longer text
- You are a developer building enterprise software
- You care about AI safety and responsible AI

Most people who use both regularly say Claude gives more thoughtful, accurate, and nuanced answers. ChatGPT is faster and has more consumer features. Both are genuinely impressive.
