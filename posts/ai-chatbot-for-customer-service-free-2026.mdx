## Best Free AI Chatbots For Customer Service In 2026

Here is an honest comparison of every major free AI chatbot available right now.

---

### 1. Chatbase — Best For Small Websites

**Free plan gives you:** 50 messages per month, 1 chatbot

Chatbase is one of the cleanest and easiest AI chatbot builders out there. You literally paste in your website URL or upload a document — and it trains an AI chatbot on your content in minutes. No coding. No technical setup.

Your visitors can then ask the chatbot questions and it answers based on what is actually on your website.

**Best for:** Small business websites, portfolios, simple service pages

**The catch:** 50 messages per month is genuinely quite low. If you get decent traffic, you will hit this limit quickly.

---

### 2. Denser.ai — Best For Document Heavy Businesses

**Free plan gives you:** 20 queries per month, basic features

Denser.ai is built for businesses that have a lot of documents — policies, product manuals, FAQs, knowledge bases. It reads all of it and turns it into a smart chatbot that can answer very detailed questions accurately.

**Best for:** Businesses with large documentation, legal firms, educational platforms

**The catch:** 20 queries per month is the lowest on this list. Really only useful for testing and very low traffic sites.

---

### 3. Tidio with Lyro AI — Best Overall Free Option

**Free plan gives you:** 50 conversations per month, live chat included

Tidio is probably the most complete free chatbot solution available right now. What makes it stand out is that it combines a traditional live chat widget with Lyro — their AI assistant. So you get the AI handling common questions automatically, but a human can jump in anytime for complex queries.

The setup is straightforward. Install a small piece of code on your website, connect Tidio, teach it about your business, and it starts working.

**Best for:** E-commerce, service businesses, anyone who wants a mix of AI and human support

**The catch:** 50 conversations per month on free. Note that it counts a full conversation — not just a message. So this goes further than it sounds.

---

### 4. Chatling — Good For Beginners

**Free plan gives you:** 35 messages per month, 1 bot

Chatling is beginner friendly. Very simple interface. You can have a chatbot live on your site in under 30 minutes without any technical knowledge. It trains on your website content automatically.

**Best for:** First time chatbot users, very small businesses just starting out

**The catch:** 35 messages per month and basic customisation on free. Limited branding control.

---

### 5. BotPenguin — Best Free Limit

**Free plan gives you:** 2,000 messages per month, basic AI and branding

This is the most generous free plan on the list by a significant margin. 2,000 messages per month is enough to handle a genuinely active small business website without paying anything.

BotPenguin works across websites, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Telegram — so your chatbot is not just on your site but wherever your customers are.

**Best for:** Growing businesses that need higher message volume without paying yet

**The catch:** The AI on the free plan is more basic compared to Chatbase or Tidio. Branding is limited — BotPenguin branding shows on your widget.

---

### 6. Zapier Chatbots — Best For Automation Lovers

**Free plan gives you:** 500 messages per month, 2 bots, basic training

If you already use Zapier to connect your apps and automate your business — their chatbot fits perfectly into that ecosystem. You can connect it to Google Sheets, your CRM, your email marketing tool, and more.

Someone fills out a form in your chatbot? It automatically adds them to your email list. They book a call? It goes straight into your calendar. That kind of automation is very powerful for a free plan.

**Best for:** Businesses already on Zapier, anyone who wants chatbot + automation together

**The catch:** The chatbot itself is less intelligent than dedicated tools like Tidio or Chatbase. The value here is the automation, not the AI itself.

## How To Actually Set One Up — Quick Steps

For most of these tools the setup follows the same basic pattern:

1. Sign up for free on their website
2. Create a new chatbot and give it a name
3. Train it — either paste your website URL or upload your FAQ document
4. Customise the colours and greeting message to match your brand
5. Copy the small code snippet they give you
6. Paste it into your website — for Next.js sites paste it in your `_app.js` inside a `<Script>` tag
7. Test it yourself before going live

The whole process takes about 30 to 45 minutes for most tools. No developer needed.
